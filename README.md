# All commands for this app

---

## For Departments:

- `Who is head of department <department_name>`
- `Show <department_name> statistics`
- `Show the average salary for the department <department_name>`
- `Show count of employee for <department_name>`
- `List departments`
- `Create department <department_name> <head_id> <lectors_id_comma_separated>`
- `Update department <id> <department_name> <head_id> <lectors_id_comma_separated>`
- `Delete department <id>`
- `Get department <id>`

---

## For Lectors:

- `List lectors`
- `Create lector <firstname> <lastname> <salary> <degree> <department>`
- `Update lector <id> <firstname> <lastname> <salary> <degree> <department>`
- `Delete lector <id>`
- `Get lector <id>`

---

## Global search:

- `Global search by <template>`

---

**Examples:**
- `Who is head of department Physics`
- `Show Physics statistics`
- `Create lector John Doe 5500 ASSISTANT Physics`
